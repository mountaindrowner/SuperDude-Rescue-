personality onto the existing cutscenes, hub banter, and ambient objective lines. All dialogue is

## §0 — ONE RECONCILIATION (so the build spec stays consistent)

**Nature-expert moves to Nayah.** Kevin is now purely the **captain / leader** (ex-Officer, super-
soldier). **Nayah** (Natrix Bernaia) is the **nature expert** *and* fearless risk-taker — a daredevil

## §1 — THE ENSEMBLE SHAPE (a nice structural detail)

The crew splits into **locals** (Adventure City history) and **outsiders** (Danny called them in):

- **Locals:** **Danny** (hometown boy-genius), **Nayah** (former mayoral candidate), **Kevin** (former city cop).
- **Outsiders Danny recruited from afar:** **Vic** (ex-botanist), **Josh** (ex-mapmaker), **Carlos** (ex-photographer/astronaut).

So "calling the crew" has shape: Danny rallies old hometown allies *and* flies in specialist friends.

**One-line plot patch** (keeps it coherent that outsiders are trapped *in* the city): the outsiders
**answered Danny's call and got swamped the moment they arrived** — caught in the flood on the way to
help. Gag-friendly: "I flew in to save the day and got bodied by a tidal wave of nachos in the parking lot."

## §2 — HERO DOSSIERS (former life → why they're perfect now → joke lines)

### TIMETECH VIC — *former botanist*
**Tie-in:** quit plants; now the world is angry plant-food. Her botany makes her lethal in the Park.
- VIC: "I quit botany to get *away* from angry vegetables. The universe has jokes."
- VIC: "I used to cross-breed tomatoes for fun. Now they'd cross-breed *me* if I stood still."
- (Hub) VIC: "Botany didn't pay. Turns out 'I make excellent killer plants' is a red flag on a résumé."

### ZOOKEEPER JOSH — *former mapmaker (didn't work out)*
**Tie-in:** a cartographer who's cheerfully *bad* at it — the running "are we lost?" gag on the big maps.
- (on a huge map) JOSH: "I used to make maps! …Badly. Switched to critters — they don't need a legend."
- JOSH: "Cartography's loss is the zoo's gain. Also, we might be lost. Kidding. …Mostly."
- (Hub) JOSH: "Mapped this whole city once. Left off three streets and a lake. Fired before lunch."

### GALAXY GUIDE CARLOS — *former field photographer → astronaut*
**Tie-in:** the photographer's eye + the astronaut's overview = he literally "sees the big picture."
- (the Tower reveal) CARLOS: "I shot war zones and I orbited the planet. You learn to see the whole frame. And the frame says: *the Tower.*"
- CARLOS: "Every good photo is about what's *outside* the shot. Same with this mess."
- (Hub) CARLOS: "From a darkroom to the dark of space. This is my first shot back on solid ground."

### CAPTAIN KEVIN — *former Officer Kevin → super-soldier program*
**Tie-in:** local rookie cop, "upgraded," now the disciplined captain who gets everyone home.
- KEVIN: "Walked this beat as a rookie. Then they… upgraded me. Same badge — bigger shoulders."
- KEVIN: "Officer, Captain, super-whatever — the job's the same: get everybody home."
- (meeting Danny) KEVIN: "You called for backup instead of playing hero solo. Good instinct, kid. Backup's here."

### NAYAH (Natrix Bernaia) — *former Mayor Nayah → lost the election to Ada Bloom*
**Tie-in:** nature expert + daredevil; a would-be mayor who traded City Hall for the wild. The Bloom
rivalry is her signature bit (§3).
- NAYAH: "I almost ran this city, y'know. Lost by *one* debate. About pigeons. ONE."
- NAYAH: "Bloom got the office. I got the wilderness. Honestly? Better view."
- (rescue) NAYAH: "The city needs saving and they sent *me*? Ha — Bloom must be *thrilled.*"

*(Danny needs no new past — he's the one who never left, which is the point: the hometown kid who
called everyone home.)*

## §3 — THE RUNNING GAG: NAYAH × MAYOR BLOOM (escalates across the game)

Ada Bloom is the story's frazzled Mayor NPC — and she beat Nayah in the election. Seed these across the
back half (Nayah joins in the Sewers/Stage 5, so the gag runs Stage 5 → finale):

1. **First contact (radio, Stage 5 or Nayah's first hub return):**
   MAYOR BLOOM: "Wait — is that *Nayah* down there?"  NAYAH: "Hi, Ada. Nice apocalypse. Very on-brand for your term."
2. **Mid-finale (grudging teamwork):**
   MAYOR BLOOM: "Nayah, whatever's between us — thank you. Truly."  NAYAH (softening): "…Don't get sappy, Mayor. But — you're welcome."
3. **Finale button (rooftop, after the win):**
   MAYOR BLOOM: "You know… maybe you should run again next term."  NAYAH (grinning at the skyline): "And leave all *this*? …I'll think about it."

Optional deep-cut: a campaign poster for "MAYOR BLOOM" as a destructible/background prop in Central
District, and a faded "NAYAH FOR MAYOR" poster half-buried in the flood somewhere — a silent visual gag.

## §4 — WHERE THE JOKES LAND (placement)

- **Rescue cutscenes** — each hero's first dossier line fits their rescue moment (the freshest, funniest beat).
- **Hub idle banter** — the "(Hub)" lines play as random one-offs when the roster is standing around the
  Central District between missions (a rotating pool; keep them rare so they stay funny).
- **Ambient objective lines** — the tie-in lines fire on relevant maps (Vic's in the Park, Josh's "are we
  lost" on any big map, Carlos's at the array).
- **The Bloom gag** runs on radio during Stages 5–6 and the finale, per §3's escalation.
- **All optional & skippable** — they're flavor. Tap-to-advance still applies; never block gameplay.

*That's the personality pass: five former lives, each feeding a gag that ties to how they play, plus the
Nayah–Bloom rivalry as the recurring comic thread. Drop into the existing dialogue system — no new tech.*
